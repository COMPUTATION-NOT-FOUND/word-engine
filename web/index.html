      How to play

      Click a tile in your rack, then a square on the board. Click a placed tile to take it back. Words read left to right or top to bottom and must join the tiles already down; the first word covers the centre star. Coloured squares double or triple a letter or the whole word. Using all seven tiles in one move scores 50 extra. The blank (shown as ?) can be any letter and scores 0.

      Drag across a word (any of the 8 directions), or tap its first and last letter.
